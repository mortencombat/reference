---
title: Privacy policy
date: 2020-03-26 20:46:04
---

This site is a static, self-hosted instance of Reference. It contains no advertisements, no affiliate links, no
analytics and no third-party tracking scripts. Nothing you do here is reported to anyone.

The site stores a single preference (your light or dark theme choice) in your browser's local storage. That value
never leaves your browser.

The operator of this instance may keep ordinary web server access logs. Ask them about their retention policy.
